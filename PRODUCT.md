# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Carter's Bakery serves two primary audiences:

- Everyday Barbadian customers who already know the bakery and want quick access to its products, prices, hours, and contact details.
- Online visitors who want to discover a trusted local bakery and understand what it offers before visiting or getting in touch.

## Product Purpose

The website builds recognition, appetite, trust, and familiarity around Carter's Bakery. It should make local customers feel that the bakery belongs to their community, while giving new visitors a clear and inviting introduction to its products and character.

Success means that visitors quickly understand what Carter's offers, trust it as an established neighbourhood bakery, and can easily browse products, plan a visit, or ask about an order.

## Brand Personality

Rooted, generous, and spirited.

Carter's should feel like a contemporary Bajan neighbourhood institution. Its character draws from Barbados's British, African, and Caribbean influences without turning them into costume or nostalgia. The voice should feel confident, familiar, welcoming, and full of life.

## Anti-references

The site must not resemble:

- Tourist-resort or souvenir-shop styling.
- Stuffy or romanticised colonial nostalgia.
- Rustic farmhouse bakery clichés.
- A generic trendy café that could belong anywhere.
- A polished startup template with interchangeable cards, typography, and copy.

## Design Principles

1. **Feel locally recognised.** Give everyday customers the feeling that this is their bakery, not a brand created for outsiders.
2. **Make heritage feel alive.** Express Bajan history and cultural mixture through confident contemporary choices, not museum-like nostalgia.
3. **Lead with generosity.** Use appetising imagery, welcoming language, and clear information to make the bakery feel abundant and approachable.
4. **Balance pomp with familiarity.** Give Carter's the presence of an institution without making it formal, distant, or precious.
5. **Help visitors act quickly.** Keep products, prices, hours, directions, and inquiry options easy to find.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain clear hierarchy, readable text, keyboard access, visible focus states, and useful reduced-motion fallbacks. The primary experience may use expressive, cinematic motion when it strengthens the brand and does not obstruct core information or actions.
